Normal evidence now offers optional source-bound alternatives for internal paragraph functions. The 53-card handbook spans eight section types and separates literal source context from authored adaptations. A route is selected by the reader task and manuscript context, then rewritten with the manuscript's facts and terminology. Keeping the original unchanged is valid. Bare writing bypasses the catalog.

Physical source hashes, exact raw spans, actual Chin Pang Ho bylines, literal phrases and anchors are checked. Held-out eligibility includes every paper seen in the route-generating consultation, not only the final example. Unknown derivations are withheld. Current clean algorithm alternatives survive the 2410 source-family holdout. Legacy imitation templates with unknown or other authorship are withheld without changing historical human decisions.

The reviewer receives the actual revised target, original target, neighboring context and complete referenced source examples. It checks joins, referents and imported scientific claims. Newly pasted declared prose slots are rejected; legitimate LaTeX optional arguments and mathematical intervals are not treated as slots. Technical copying is assessed contextually; no overlap quota or forced rewrite is imposed.

A separate exact human supplement now supplies filtered approved schemas and terminology contexts. Its eight-schema and twenty-context decisions do not approve new route adaptations or scientific claims. Literal frequency, reviewed applicability, source authorship and measured writing quality remain separate. Existing scientific knowledge keeps source-specific attribution.
