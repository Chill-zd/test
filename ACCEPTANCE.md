## Required real acceptance protocol

Level 1: verify the complete intended real knowledge corpus with drop rate below 20%; require at least 15 shared lexicon entries supported by three or more distinct papers. The current shared-entry count is 15; assess its retained exact source support separately from semantic applicability and the remaining corpus/drop-rate requirements. All section schemas must be reviewed by a person and marked `approved` before Level 3. Source projection and quote checks do not establish semantic or stylistic validity.

Level 2: run the offline manuscript pipeline and injected bad-citation, changed-mathematics and missing-annotation paths; retain failed candidates and audits, generate separate scientific questions, and deliver no patch with failed checks, explicit escalation or unresolved new scientific meaning. An inherited verification question alone does not block an otherwise faithful, reviewed language edit. Current tests cover these engineering paths. Real extraction and retrieval checks are additional evidence, not replacements for the protocol.

Level 3: prepare two genuine tasks in each of the eight section types excluding `appendix` and `unknown`, and run full and bare variants against the same source snapshot. Human review checks source support (three sampled changes), substantive annotation fulfillment, preservation of conditions/citations/scientific meaning, and attribution accuracy. Fabricated citations, silently altered scientific meaning, silently dropped annotations or compilation regressions are hard failures. Require at least six section categories entirely green, zero hard failures, and identifiable evidence-based style improvements in at least five categories.

Two formal Level 3 rounds failing at the same stage trigger the specified evidence-package plus human-writing fallback. The diagnostic/clean-input engineering pilots are not formal Level 3 rounds and do not trigger that conclusion.

Raw source text and hashes remain authoritative for patches; only comment-free prose projections enter knowledge extraction and source-evidence retrieval.
